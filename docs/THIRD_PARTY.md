# Distribution dependencies

The desktop uses Electron, React, Three.js, React Three Fiber/Drei, Framer Motion, Tailwind, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, keyring, FFmpeg, faster-whisper, CTranslate2, PyAV and PySceneDetect, plus their dependencies. Their license terms apply to redistribution. Python and Electron dependency notices are retained in the packaged distributions where supplied by their installers/bundlers.

The bundled imageio-ffmpeg binary includes FFmpeg and libx264. Review its build configuration and applicable GPL/source redistribution obligations before commercial redistribution. Source and build references: https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg and https://ffmpeg.org/download.html . Do not remove bundled license files. This project does not grant rights to third-party video, audio, speech models, fonts or business data.

Background music is supplied by the owner. Import requires an explicit declaration of ownership or a valid license; the application ships no commercial music tracks. Whisper model weights download from the selected upstream model repository and have their own model terms. Hosted AI, search and Gmail use each provider's terms, quotas and billing.
